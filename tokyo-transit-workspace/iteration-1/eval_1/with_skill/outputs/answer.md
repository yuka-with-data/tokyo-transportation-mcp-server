# Tokyo Station to Shinjuku Station
The simplest way is the JR Chuo Line.
- From Tokyo Station, take the JR Chuo Rapid Line toward Shinjuku / Nakano / Takao.
- Ride to Shinjuku Station.
- Typical travel time is about 15 to 20 minutes.

Alternative:
- You can also take the Tokyo Metro Marunouchi Line from Tokyo Station to Shinjuku, but JR Chuo Rapid is usually the easiest option.
