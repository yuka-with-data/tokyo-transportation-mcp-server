# Tokyo Station to Shinjuku Station
The simplest route is to take the JR Chuo Rapid Line.

- Start at Tokyo Station.
- Board the JR Chuo Rapid Line toward Shinjuku / Nakano / Takao.
- Get off at Shinjuku Station.
- Typical travel time is about 15 to 20 minutes.

## Alternative:
- The Tokyo Metro Marunouchi Line also connects Tokyo Station and Shinjuku Station.
