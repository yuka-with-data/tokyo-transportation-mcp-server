# Shinjuku Station to Tokyo Tower

Best route: take the **Toei Oedo Line** from **Shinjuku** to **Akabanebashi**, then walk to Tokyo Tower.

- **Transfers:** 0
- **Train time:** 12 minutes
- **Train fare:** 220 yen
- **Walking segment:** about 7 minutes from **Akabanebashi** to **Tokyo Tower**
- **Total travel time:** about 19 minutes
- **Total fare:** 220 yen

Route summary:
1. **Shinjuku → Akabanebashi** on the Toei Oedo Line
2. **Walk from Akabanebashi to Tokyo Tower**

This is the best option returned by the Tokyo Transit MCP tools because it avoids transfers and keeps both the fare and total travel time low.
